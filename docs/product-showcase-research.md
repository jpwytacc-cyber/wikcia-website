# Product-led showcase research

## Brief and decision
Preserve the approved paper/ink/citron identity. Make the product proposition explicit: **Wikcia Studio is AI agentic software for online marketing, outreach and web design**, with the Wikcia assistant connecting research, preparation, creative production and engineering. Shift the first half of the homepage from a general creative-assistant narrative to product evidence.

## Public references inspected
- [Gumloop](https://www.gumloop.com/): puts a concrete agent task, its working context, artifact and approval boundary next to the product proposition. Useful principle: explain the task through an inspectable example, not only a feature list. Its customer metrics and integration counts are not evidence about Wikcia and will not be reused.
- [Lindy](https://www.lindy.ai/): introduces the assistant in human language and pairs the introduction with a specific work scenario and visible deliverable. Useful principle: show what the user asks, what the assistant prepares and where the result appears. Do not borrow its privacy, compliance, integration or customer claims.
- [Relay](https://www.relay.app/): its current official site says the service shut down in September 2026. Rejected as a current product reference despite appearing in search results. This is why official pages were checked rather than trusting comparison articles.

## Product evidence inspected locally
The actual Wikcia Desktop source and built renderer identify the product as Wikcia Studio and expose native navigation for Chat, Studio, Agents, Projects, Tools, Automations and Apps. The shell has conversation and intelligence panels, model/provider context, task visibility and application routes. These are interface observations, not proof of every tool's operational readiness.

Two public-ready **actual interface crops** were selected: a conversation workspace detail and the Creative Studio tool-discovery view. The latter shows per-application states and explicitly says discovery does not establish readiness. Captures are from local development builds. Private sidebars, conversations and account context are excluded by cropping; no private full-screen capture is published. Images are re-encoded without EXIF metadata. Local source and asset hashes are retained in the private QA provenance manifest.

Only independently reviewed, non-sensitive interface crops are included. The original private capture material is not part of the public website. Interface imagery is evidence of the product appearance, not a claim that every displayed integration has been exercised. No installed provider settings, app source or credentials were changed for the website.

## Implementation requirements
1. Introduce Wikcia in first person and name marketing, outreach and web design above the fold.
2. Bring real product screenshots close to the introduction; provide an accessible enlargement view with truthful captions.
3. Add a guided, interactive product example using a fictional project. Preserve a visible **sample walkthrough / no live AI** boundary. Let visitors inspect steps, edit a draft, mark an example approval and download the actual current example brief. Never imply that outreach was sent, a campaign launched or a service connected.
4. Add visuals of deliverables: actual studio concept sites, a campaign direction and a reviewable outreach draft. Do not invent client results, contact lists or marketing metrics.
5. Improve the existing ribbon, not replace its identity. Place it against a dark graphite exhibition surface; retain readable independent controls. Add horizontal drag/touch rotation, arrow-key rotation, and an intentional accent/material choice, with a designed vector fallback. Keep vertical touch scrolling available and preserve reduced-motion/lifecycle behavior.
6. Verify screenshots, display contrast, real rendering changes, touch/keyboard controls, demo state and exports, privacy boundaries and representative widths before the GitHub/Vercel release.
